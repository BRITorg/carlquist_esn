About This Project

Sherwin Carlquist (1930–2021) was one of the preeminent botanists of the twentieth century, renowned for his foundational contributions to wood anatomy, island biogeography, and plant systematics. Over a career spanning more than six decades, he authored 343 scientific publications and developed methodologies that continue to shape how researchers understand plant evolution on islands and the comparative anatomy of flowering plants.

The Sherwin Carlquist Digital Extended Specimen Network links physical botanical specimens, field photography, published research, and archival materials into a unified, interoperable digital resource. Anchored by a comprehensive publications dataset and supported by Wikidata integration, the network makes Carlquist's scientific legacy discoverable, citable, and machine-readable.

The project is a collaboration between the Botanical Research Institute of Texas (BRIT) at the Fort Worth Botanic Garden and California Botanic Garden, funded by the National Science Foundation (Award 2133562).

Publications Dataset

📄

carlquist_publications.csv

Main bibliographic file. 343 records, 20 standardized fields following CSL-JSON and Dublin Core conventions. Includes titles, dates, DOIs, ISSNs, and Wikidata QIDs for all records.

📰

carlquist_journals.csv

Companion lookup table for the 48 journals in which Carlquist published. Includes print and electronic ISSNs and Wikidata QIDs for each journal.

👥

carlquist_authors.csv

Companion lookup table for 73 co-authors, recording name variants as cited alongside full names and Wikidata identifiers where available.
